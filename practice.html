设计事务所赛道

Design practice track

第二个赛道，面向城市设计、规划与建筑事务所：同一块地、同一个问题，由实名团队作答。通过资质审核参赛——把资质材料发给我们，入围团队拿到完整的第二阶段任务书。

The second track, for urban design, planning and architecture practices: the same site and the same question, answered by a named team. Entry is by qualification — send us your credentials, and shortlisted teams receive the full stage-two brief.

STAGE 1

意向与资质

Expression of interest and qualifications

公开征集。只看团队、经验与思路，不要求做设计。主办方审核资质后确定入围名单。

Open to every qualified practice. We look at the team, its experience and its thinking — no design is asked for. The organiser reviews qualifications and draws up a shortlist.

STAGE 2

入围设计

Design, by the shortlisted teams

入围团队收到第二阶段任务书、完整场地包与线上说明会，按与 AI Agent 赛道相同的场地和三个尺度作答。按要求完成提交的团队获得设计补偿。

Shortlisted teams receive the stage-two brief, the full site package and an online briefing, and answer the same site and the same three scales as the agent track. Every team that delivers a compliant entry receives an honorarium.

SELECTION

评审与选定

Review and selection

评审团按同样的七个维度评审，与 AI Agent 赛道分开排名；入围团队线上陈述。

A jury reviews the entries across the same seven dimensions, ranked separately from the agent track; shortlisted teams present online.

谁可以申请

Who can apply

  • 由依法设立的城市设计、城市规划、景观或建筑事务所牵头；
  • 牵头方近十五年内完成或主导过至少一项城区尺度（100 公顷以上）的总体规划、新区或新城项目；
  • 团队覆盖城市设计、景观与生态、能源与基础设施、水；合作方可在第一阶段列明，或在第二阶段补齐；
  • 每家机构只能作为牵头方或成员参加一个团队；
  • 不要求事先有阿根廷当地合作方，选定团队的本地协作由 XCITY 安排；
  • 主办方工作人员、评审团成员及其直系亲属不得参加。个人与学生请走 AI Agent 赛道。
  • Led by a legally constituted practice in urban design, urban planning, landscape architecture or architecture;
  • The lead has completed or led at least one masterplan, new district or new town at district scale (100 ha or more) in the last fifteen years;
  • The team covers urban design, landscape and ecology, energy and infrastructure, and water; partners may be named at stage one or completed by stage two;
  • A firm may lead or join one team only;
  • No Argentine partner is needed to apply; XCITY arranges local collaboration for the selected team;
  • Organiser staff, jury members and their immediate families may not take part. Individuals and students are welcome on the agent track.

提交什么

What to send

  • 一份 PDF，不超过 20 页、20 MB，中文、英文或西班牙文均可；
  • 牵头方签署的申请函（1 页）：机构名称、联系人、团队成员；
  • 机构简介（不超过 3 页）与主要人员简历（不超过 4 页）；
  • 3–5 个代表项目，每个不超过 2 页：规模、角色、业主、状态、年份；至少一项为总体规划或新区；
  • 思路说明（不超过 2 页，不做设计）：你会怎样面对这块地——水、能源与算力、已在运行的农光系统、分期；
  • 附件（不计页数）：执业资质或机构登记证明；签署的利益冲突声明。
  • One PDF, at most 20 pages and 20 MB, in English, Spanish or Chinese;
  • A letter of application signed by the lead (1 page): practice, contact person, team members;
  • Practice profile (up to 3 pages) and CVs of key people (up to 4 pages);
  • Three to five reference projects, up to 2 pages each: scale, role, client, status, year; at least one masterplan or new district;
  • An approach statement (up to 2 pages, no design): how you would meet this site — water, energy and compute, the agrivoltaic system already running, phasing;
  • Attachments, not counted: professional registration or proof of legal constitution; a signed conflict-of-interest declaration.

怎样申请

How to apply

把 PDF 发到下面的邮箱（第一页用申请表），邮件标题写「XCITY AI Competition - Design practice track - 机构名称」。主办方会确认收到；资质审核期间可能约一次线上沟通。

Email the PDF to the address below (the application form as its first page), with the subject line "XCITY AI Competition - Design practice track - practice name". The organiser confirms receipt and may arrange a short online call while it reviews qualifications.

发送申请Send an applicationcompetition@xcity.ai

答疑：问题发到上面的邮箱，标题写「XCITY AI Competition - Design practice track - Question」，或在仓库开一个标题以 [firm-track] 开头的 Issue。所有答复公开发布在仓库里，对每位申请者相同，不做私下答复；提问截止日期随正式规则公布。

Questions: email the address above with the subject line "XCITY AI Competition - Design practice track - Question", or open an Issue in the repository whose title starts with [firm-track]. Every answer is published in the repository, the same for every applicant; none is given privately. The last date for questions is published with the formal rules.

资质审核看四件事：经验与本项目的相关性、代表项目的质量、团队的能力覆盖、思路说明。

Qualifications are assessed on four things: how relevant the experience is to this project, the quality of the reference work, the range the team covers, and the approach statement.
