设计任务

Design brief

寻找能提出有空间品质、值得继续委托深化的城市设计团队。两个阶段：先资格审核与初步想法，入围后再做完整设计，完整设计有补偿。

We are looking for teams that can propose urban design of real spatial quality, worth commissioning further. Two stages: qualifications and initial ideas first; the shortlist then designs in full, and is paid for it.

STAGE 1

资格与初步想法

Qualifications and initial ideas

公开征集，免费。先审资格；通过的机构提交不超过 10 页的初步想法，草图与示意即可，不要求完整方案。主办方结合想法、作品与团队评出入围名单。

Open and free. Qualifications are reviewed first; practices that pass send initial ideas of up to 10 pages — sketches and diagrams, not a full scheme. The organiser draws up the shortlist from the ideas, the work and the team.

STAGE 2

入围设计

Design, by the shortlisted teams

入围团队拿到完整场地包、线上说明会与书面答疑期，完成四个层次：全域空间结构、一期总体布局、城市设计放大与重点议题。按要求完成提交的团队获得设计补偿。

Shortlisted teams receive the full site package, an online briefing and a written question period, and design four layers: the whole site, phase one, an urban-design zoom-in and the key issues. Every team that delivers a compliant entry receives an honorarium.

SELECTION

评审与选定

Review and selection

入围团队向评审团线上汇报，评审按六项标准进行（见「评审标准」）。选定团队与 XCITY 进入深化合作。

Shortlisted teams present online; the jury scores the entries on six criteria (see Evaluation). Selected teams move to further work with XCITY.

1 · 全域空间结构1 · The whole site完整覆盖 1,656 km²：哪里保护、生产、建城；中心、走廊、容量与分期（十项决定）All 1,656 km²: where to protect, produce and build; centres, corridors, capacity and phasing (ten decisions)
2 · 一期总体布局2 · Phase one由团队划定：城市从哪里开始，如何独立运行。参考情景 5 万人 · 2 万岗位 · 200 MW · 1–3 GWDrawn by the team: where the city starts and how it runs on its own. Reference scenario 50,000 people · 20,000 jobs · 200 MW · 1–3 GW
3 · 城市设计放大3 · Urban design zoom-in一期中自选地区，不固定面积：街区、体量、公共空间与导则An area of phase one chosen by the team, no fixed size: blocks, massing, public space and guidelines
4 · 重点设计议题4 · Key issues六项选三项深化，其余八项作策略回应Three of six in depth; the other eight in principle

不符合要求：只把一个片区做细、其余场地只有色块示意的方案。

Not suitable: a scheme that designs one district and leaves the rest of the site as a colour diagram.

场地与系统

Site and systems

一种未来，是向外寻找新的世界。XCITY 选择先向内看——在地球上，仍有大量土地、能源与生产能力，尚未被重新组织成适应 AI 时代的城市系统。
San Juan 提供了这样一个真实起点。两个征集的场地是两块相连的土地——共 1,656 km²：大尺度连续土地、充足的太阳能资源、已经在运行的农光系统；但城市也必须回应这里真实的边界——有限的水资源、Pie de Palo 山地、地震、Zonda 风与干河床，而且场内没有既有国道，最近的 RN 40 在约 28 km 外。
所以，这里既不是一张可以任意想象的白纸，也不是一道只需要画出「未来感」的题目。设计要做的，是在能源、生产、生活与这些真实约束之间，建立一套成立的城市关系。

One future looks outward for a new world. XCITY looks inward first: there is still a great deal of land, energy and productive capacity on this planet that has not been reorganised into urban systems fit for the age of AI.
San Juan offers one such starting point. The site of both calls is two joined pieces of land — 1,656 km²: large continuous land, an abundant solar resource, an agrivoltaic system already running — against real limits the city has to answer to: a constrained water supply, the Pie de Palo range, seismic risk, the Zonda and dry riverbeds, and no existing national road on site, the nearest, RN 40, about 28 km away.
So this is neither a blank sheet to imagine freely on, nor a brief that only asks for the look of the future. What a design has to do is establish a city that holds together between energy, production, daily life and these real constraints.

场地——共 1,656 km²，连成一片；试点区——三片光伏农业区（约 14 km²）与生活区一期（约 2.5 km²），由 XCITY 规划，不在设计范围内；其余土地全部开放设计。点选右侧任一城市系统，查看它的意向位置——表达规划方向，不是用地边界。
居住点位为一期量级的示意分布，远期规模与聚落数量由方案提出。Site — 1,656 km² in one piece. Pilot area — three agrivoltaic fields (about 14 km²) and the first living quarter (about 2.5 km²), planned by XCITY and not open to design; everything else is open to design. Open any system in the list to see where it leans — a direction, not a land boundary.
The living dots are indicative of phase-one scale; the long-term size and number of settlements are for entries to propose.

五个城市系统

Five urban systems

五个系统可以互相重叠——占比代表主要规划权重，不是完全互斥的用地边界。

The five systems overlap. The shares are the weight each carries in the plan, not mutually exclusive land boundaries.

选址建议Siting优先选择坡度较缓、非洪泛、农业价值较低，并具备电网、通信与冷却条件的区域；算力设施与住宅之间需要合理的噪声、热环境与运维缓冲。Gentler slopes, clear of flood, of lower agricultural value, with access to grid, communications and cooling; a workable noise, thermal and maintenance buffer between compute and housing.

阳光 → 电力 → 算力。这是这座城市的生产底座：光伏发电，电力不外售，转化为算力；算力对内调度农业与城市运行，对外输出服务。
取水、冷却与余热去向，是这一系统真正的选址约束；而它落在哪里，也决定了其余四个系统还能落在哪里。

Sunlight → power → compute. The productive base of the city: the array generates, none of it is sold onward, and it becomes compute — which runs the farm and the city from within and sells services outward.
Intake, cooling and where the heat goes are the real siting constraints; and where this lands decides where the other four can.

选址建议Siting基地北部与中部的河流谷地及平坦地区，依托安第斯山融雪水系。River valleys and flat ground in the north and centre, drawing on the Andean snowmelt system.

板上发电、板下种植、地面放牧——同一片土地三层用途。板下是既有的共生系统：霍霍巴、迷迭香、饲用仙人掌与羊驼，排布不得改动。开心果、橄榄、葡萄等高株与藤本作物不能进入板阵，位置与规模由方案提出。
系统已经在运行，城市要长在它旁边：两者之间那条边如何组织、随城市成长如何移动，是这一层要回答的。

Power above, planting below, grazing on the ground — one piece of land doing three things. Under the arrays, an existing symbiotic system: jojoba, rosemary, forage cactus and alpaca; its layout is fixed. Tall and climbing crops — pistachio, olive, vine — cannot enter the array; where they go and how much is for entries to propose.
The system is already running and the city grows beside it. How that edge is organised, and how it moves as the city grows, is the question here.

选址建议Siting靠近生活基础设施与可靠水源，避开洪泛、重型物流与算力设施的直接影响，与生产设施保持合理缓冲。Near everyday infrastructure and a reliable water source; clear of flood, of heavy logistics and of direct effects from the compute facilities, with a workable buffer to production.

一座由人类居住、AI Agent 协调、机器人参与运行的城市。
Human — 创造 / 判断 / 生活　Agent — 推理 / 协调 / 调度　Robot — 执行 / 生产 / 维护

两种住法，比重与位置由方案决定：
群居——聚落与城区。统一的高度与建筑秩序，以共享的步行空间连接住户；学校、诊所、商店与街道在这一类里才成立，公共生活发生在这里。
庄园——围绕私人土地展开的生活。独栋低密度，自带种植或岩体地形，分布在山前缓坡与种植区边缘；占地快，须说明用地量。

当通勤不再是组织生活的轴心，人如何聚居——聚成一座城，还是沿谷地形成若干聚落——由方案回答。

A city inhabited by people, coordinated by AI agents, operated in part by robots.
Human — making, judgement, living.　Agent — reasoning, coordination, scheduling.　Robot — execution, production, maintenance.

Two ways of living; their mix and location are the scheme's to decide:
Clustered — settlements and town centre. A consistent height and building order, dwellings linked by shared pedestrian space; school, clinic, shop and street only become possible at this density. Public life happens here.
Estate — life organised around a private piece of land. Detached and low density, with its own planting or rock terrain, on the lower slopes and at the edge of the planted ground; it consumes land quickly, so state how much.

Once commuting is no longer the axis, how people settle — one city, or several settlements along the valleys — is the scheme's to answer.

选址建议Siting产业指生产、加工与维修，物流指运输、仓储与转运。聚落型产业：农产品加工、光伏与机器人运维、本地制造，以及场内物流（材料堆场、农产品集散、场内配送），随各期聚落布置，每一期都有。门户型产业：出口制造，与对外贸易与口岸相邻，位于场地东翼、Pie de Palo 山地东侧的平地，山体东侧为背风面，避 Zonda 与西来沙尘。场内无既有国道，东翼距 RP 510 约 42 km（场地边界最近处约 35.6 km），进场通道须在一期列入工程量。后期建设，一期预留用地。Industry means making, processing and repair; logistics means moving, storing and transfer. Settlement industry: agricultural processing, solar and robot maintenance, local making, plus on-site logistics (material yards, produce collection, local delivery), sits with each phase's settlements, so every phase has some. Gateway industry: export manufacturing beside the port of entry, on the flat ground of the site's eastern wing, east of the Pie de Palo range — the lee side, sheltered from the Zonda and from dust off the west. There is no existing national road on site: RP 510 is about 42 km from the eastern wing (35.6 km from the nearest point of the boundary), so the access corridor is phase-one construction work. Built in a later phase with its land held from phase one.

制造与农产品加工——农业系统的产出在这里变成可出口的商品。
自动运输是需要纳入的设计条件：在货运不再由人驾驶的前提下，道路断面、装卸方式与人车关系应该怎么重新组织。

Manufacturing and agricultural processing — where what the fields produce becomes something that can be shipped.
Autonomous freight is a design condition here: with no driver in the cab, how road sections, loading and the relation between people and vehicles should be organised again.

选址建议Siting对外物流与贸易：货运场站、集装箱堆场、海关监管与保税仓、口岸、自由贸易。位于场地东翼、Pie de Palo 山地东侧的平地，面向东向内陆与大西洋方向的出口通道，不依赖智利山口；与门户型产业相邻。这一侧成片的平地约 7 km²，口岸与门户型产业共用。场内无既有国道，东翼距 RP 510 约 42 km（场地边界最近处约 35.6 km），进场通道须在一期列入工程量。后期建设，一期预留用地与通道。External logistics and trade: freight terminal, container yard, bonded and customs warehousing, the border crossing and free trade. On the flat ground of the site's eastern wing, east of the Pie de Palo range, facing the eastward route inland and to the Atlantic rather than the Chilean pass, next to the gateway industry. There is only about 7 km² of continuous flat ground on this side, shared with the gateway industry. There is no existing national road on site: RP 510 is about 42 km from the eastern wing (35.6 km from the nearest point of the boundary), so the access corridor is phase-one construction work. Built later, with land and access held from phase one.

为未来跨境物流、仓储、海关及自由贸易功能预留的对外口岸。面向东向内陆与大西洋方向的出口通道，在这里落地。
口岸、堆场与城市之间的空间关系，决定这座城市呈现给外部的第一个面貌。

An international gateway held for future cross-border logistics, warehousing, customs and free-trade functions. The eastward corridor inland and to Argentina's Atlantic coast lands here.
How the crossing, the yards and the city stand in relation to one another is the first face this city shows.

五个系统互为条件：任一个的位置变动，其余四个的推导都要重做。

The five are conditions on one another: move any one and the other four are derived again.

提交要求

Submission requirements

第一阶段交资格材料与初步想法；完整方案由入围团队在第二阶段提交。

Stage one asks for qualifications and initial ideas; the full scheme is submitted by the shortlisted teams in stage two.

第一阶段 · 资格与初步想法（所有申请机构）

Stage one · qualifications and initial ideas (every applicant)

  • 一份 PDF，不超过 20 页、20 MB；英文或中文
  • 申请表（第 1 页，计入 20 页，由牵头方签署）
  • 机构简介，不超过 3 页；主要人员简历，不超过 4 页
  • 3–5 个代表项目，每个不超过 2 页；至少一项为总体规划或新区
  • 另附、不计页数：执业资质或机构登记证明；签署的利益冲突声明
  • 资格审核通过后，提交初步想法，不超过 10 页（可由 PPT 导出）：场地判断与核心问题、全域与五大系统、一期启动逻辑、一个代表性空间想法、方法与专业协作
  • 初步想法用草图、示意图与关系图即可；不要求总平面、工程测算或效果图，做了也不加分
  • One PDF, at most 20 pages and 20 MB; English or Chinese
  • The application form (page 1, counted in the 20, signed by the lead)
  • Practice profile, up to 3 pages; CVs of key people, up to 4 pages
  • Three to five reference projects, up to 2 pages each; at least one masterplan or new district
  • Attached separately, not counted: proof of registration or legal constitution; a signed conflict-of-interest declaration
  • Once qualified: initial ideas, up to 10 pages (a slide deck is fine): the reading of the site, the whole site and the five systems, how phase one starts, one representative spatial idea, method and team
  • Sketches, diagrams and relationship drawings are enough; no masterplan, cost estimate or rendering is asked for, and none earns marks

第二阶段 · 方案成果（入围团队）

Stage two · the scheme (shortlisted teams)

  • 6 张 A0 横版展板：图纸、分析图、效果图与说明文字
  • 1 张预览图，用于网站与宣传
  • 设计说明，不超过 800 词：理念、场地回应与设计方法
  • A3 文本，不超过 60 页（含附录），附「要求—图纸」索引
  • 指标工作表（XLSX，注明单位、范围与依据）与 CAD / GIS 文件
  • 全部为英文；展板匿名，不得出现机构名称、标志、邮箱或其他可识别信息
  • 不要求视频与实体模型，可选；页数或媒介多不加分
  • Six A0 landscape boards: drawings, diagrams, visualisations and explanatory text
  • One preview image for the website and publicity
  • A design statement of up to 800 words: concept, response to the site and approach
  • An A3 report, up to 60 pages including appendices, indexed from each requirement to its drawing
  • A metrics workbook (XLSX: unit, scope and basis of every figure) and CAD / GIS files
  • Everything in English; boards anonymous, with no practice name, logo, email or other identifying information
  • No video or physical model required; both optional. More pages or media earn no marks

Three steps: download the form, put the material together, register interest. The deadline is published with the formal rules.

谁可以申请

Who can apply

  • 由依法设立的城市设计、城市规划、景观或建筑事务所牵头；
  • 牵头方近十五年内完成或主导过至少一项城区尺度（100 公顷以上）的总体规划、新区或新城项目；
  • 团队覆盖城市设计、景观与生态、能源与基础设施、水；合作方可在第一阶段列明，或在第二阶段补齐；
  • 每家机构只能作为牵头方或成员参加一个团队；
